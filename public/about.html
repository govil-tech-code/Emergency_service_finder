WHO WE ARE

Emergency assistance, just a click away.

Emergency Service Finder is a web-based platform designed to help people quickly locate essential emergency services in their nearby area.

During an emergency, finding the right service quickly can make a significant difference. Our platform brings hospitals, ambulances, police stations, fire stations, blood banks and pharmacies together in one convenient place.

Users can search for services based on their location and view nearby facilities through an interactive map.
